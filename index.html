Our Ethos

Rooted in Craftsmanship & Landscape

Surrounded by woodland and rolling hills, it blends a rustic, community-focused atmosphere with a deep connection to the regional landscape.

An Immersive Environment for Creatives

Cheviot Artist Retreat is an artist-led initiative born from months of immersive time spent responding to the inspiring landscape surrounding the Scottish Borders.

Founded in collaboration with the owners of Cheviot Glamping, our program harnesses this remarkable environment to offer fellow creatives space to gather inspiration and develop their work. We provide dedicated custom units as studio spaces alongside single pods for resident accommodation.

Committed to meaningful community connection, we host open studios and local exhibition opportunities that bridge visiting artists with the wider creative community in the region.
