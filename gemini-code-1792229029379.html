Rooted in Craftsmanship

An Artist-Led Collaboration with Cheviot Glamping & Brewery

Cheviot Artist Retreat is born from an intimate, responsive relationship with the surrounding Scottish Borders and Northumberland terrain. Founded in close collaboration with the team behind Cheviot Glamping, our residency program provides immersive environment for visual artists, makers, and writers.

Anchored alongside Cheviot Brewery and the onsite Cheviot Tap bar, the site is a warm hub built using materials sourced directly from the surrounding Ford & Etal Estate.
